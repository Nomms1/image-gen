Prompt Helper

Good vs Bad prompts

Good: “A young woman with long red hair standing on a cliff at sunset, wearing a flowing blue dress, soft golden light, cinematic”

Bad: “girl pretty nice background cool”

The good one tells the AI who, what they’re doing, where, and how it looks.

What to include for characters

  • Age / gender / appearance (hair, eyes, expression)
  • Clothing and style
  • Pose or action
  • Mood

What to include for the background

  • Location
  • Time of day / lighting
  • Atmosphere
  • Camera feel

Extra tips

  • Be specific but not overly long
  • Describe what you want to see
  • Art style words help a lot
